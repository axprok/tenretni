Blat

Blat refers to the use of personal networks, informal contacts, and connections to obtain goods, services, or privileges in short supply and to bypass official, bureaucratic procedures.

Survival Mechanism: During the Soviet era's chronic shortages and state rationing, blat evolved from a background practice into a shadow economy of favors—an essential system for everyday survival and problem-solving.

Types of Ties: Ledeneva distinguishes between different forms of blat based on the nature of the relationship, ranging from direct mutual utility and exchanges to deep personal bonds of friendship or kinship (svoi — "one's own people").
