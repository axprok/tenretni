Cossack States

  • Cossack community in Ukraine is an early modern state, a warrior democracy that emerged in the 1500s. They resided along the Dnieper river
  • Cossacks formed a state community/quasi state called "hosts." The most famous host is the Zaporizhian Sich (emerged around 1552).
  • 1648: Under Bohdan Khmelnytsky, the Cossacks led a major uprising against Polish-Lithuanian rule. This marked a decisive turning point in Ukrainian history and the emergence of the Cossack Hetmanate.
  • Don cossacks, Yaik Cossacks, Amur Cossacks all had their own states called hosts: The Don Cossack Host, The Amur Cossack Host, etc.
