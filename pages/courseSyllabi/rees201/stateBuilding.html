State Building

State-building is the structural and institutional project of developing formal governmental capacity, administrative systems, and coercive authority.

Nation-building is the cultural and psychological project of fostering a shared collective identity, national unity, and civic loyalty among a population (often through education, common languages, and national symbols)

Key Elements of State building.
1 Monopoly on Violence: Claiming legitimate control over instruments of force and security, typically via a standing military and police force.

2 Rule of Law and Legal Frameworks: Developing formal judicial systems, standard legal procedures, and legislative bodies to make and interpret laws.
