Waves of Migration from the USSR as Russians Remember Them

Before jumping at the five waves of emigration as Russians remember them, one point. In their 2023 book Making National Diasporas: Soviet-Era Migrations and Post-Soviet Consequences, Siegelbaum and Moch focus on broader questions of migrations, not a theory of successive waves of emigration from the USSR to the West. Their work takes a broader view of migration, encompassing
1 forced deportation,
2 internal resettlement,
3 migration between Soviet republics, and finally
4 emigration abroad.
