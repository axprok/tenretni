Ali and Nino as a Vehicle of National Cinema

Ali and Nino can be understood as a national cinema film not simply because it represents Azerbaijan, but because it participates in the process that Benedict Anderson describes as the imagining of the nation. Through landscape, history, cultural traditions, religious difference, and the struggle for political sovereignty, the film transforms Azerbaijan from a geographical and imperial space into an imagined national community.

national cinema in case of the film is vehicle of a cultural technology for imagining the nation.
