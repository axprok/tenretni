Polish Cinema

In Ashes and Diamonds (1958):
Maciek Chełmicki is a former Home Army fighter ordered to assassinate a communist official. His personal dilemma reflects the tragedy of Polish resistance fighters who found themselves on the losing side of the emerging postwar political order.

Ashes & Diamonds (Wajda 1958)

Polish Film School (Polish: polska szkoła filmowa) refers to an informal group of Polish film directors and screenplay writers active between 1956 and approximately 1963. Polish art cinema. Destalinization of Polish cinema.
