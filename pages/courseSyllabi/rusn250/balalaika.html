Balalaika

A balalaika (балалайка) is a traditional Russian three-stringed, triangular-shaped plucked instrument. It became one of the most recognizable symbols of Russian folk music.

Accompaniment: Traditionally, the balalaika accompanies folk songs, dancing, and storytelling. Its rhythmic strumming can support a singer or a group of dancers.
Rhythm: The instrument is particularly effective for creating lively, repetitive rhythmic patterns. This makes it well suited to dance music.

The balalaika is interesting because it illustrates the difference between folk tradition and national cultural symbolism. The instrument existed in village musical culture, but in the late 19th and early 20th centuries it was also standardized and transformed into a symbol of Russian national culture, especially through organized folk ensembles.
