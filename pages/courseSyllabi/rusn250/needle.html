Needle as a Magic Object in Folk Tales

In Russian folklore, the needle (игла́) is a powerful symbolic object, often associated with life, death, vulnerability, and the hidden source of power.

    The most famous example is the tale of Кощей Бессмертный (Koschei the Deathless). Koschei's death is hidden inside a series of objects:
  1. On an island stands an oak tree.
    2. Beneath the oak is a chest. Inside the chest is a hare. Inside the hare is a duck. Inside the duck is an egg. Inside the egg is a needle. The tip of the needle contains Koschei's death.
