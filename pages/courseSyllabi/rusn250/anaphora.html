Anaphora

Anaphora is a literary device in which the same word or phrase is repeated at the beginning of successive lines, phrases, or sentences. In Russian folk poetry, anaphora is often used to create rhythm, emphasize emotions, and make songs and poems easier to remember and recite.

Example from Russian folk poetry:


Oh, my field, my open field,
Oh, my little oak grove, so green,
Oh, my little river, flowing swiftly…

Уж ты, поле моё, поле чистое,
Уж ты, дубравушка зелёная,
Уж ты, реченька быстрая…

Explanation: The repeated phrase «Уж ты» (“Oh, you” or “O thou”) at the beginning of each line is an example of anaphora. This repetition gives the verse a musical quality, emphasizes the speaker’s emotional connection to nature, and reflects the characteristic style of Russian folk songs.
