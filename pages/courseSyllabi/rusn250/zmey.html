Zmey Gorynych (Змей Горыныч; literally "Dragon, Son of a Mountain") is a multi-headed, fire-breathing dragon from East Slavic folklore.

  • Multiple heads – Usually three, six, nine, or twelve heads, depending on the tale.
  • Fire-breathing – Breathes fire and can burn villages, forests, and entire armies.
  • Enormous size and supernatural strength – Possesses extraordinary physical power and is difficult to defeat.
  • Flight – Has wings and can fly, often appearing suddenly in the sky.
  • Regeneration – In some versions, severed heads can grow back, making him especially difficult to kill.
  • Abduction – Often kidnaps princesses or other women and takes them to his lair.
  • A guardian of the borderlands – Frequently lives beyond a river or in a distant, dangerous territory, symbolizing the boundary between the familiar world and the world of monsters.
  • Speech and intelligence – Unlike an ordinary animal, he can speak, threaten, bargain, and sometimes deceive his opponents.
