Structuralist Approach to Folk Tales

  • Structuralism. Vladimir Propp’s approach and how to apply it to tales
  • The key concept is the function: A function is an act of a character, defined from the point of view of its significance for the course of the action.
  • Sequence of functions is always identical.
  • All wonder tales are of one type in regard to their structure.
