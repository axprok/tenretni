Double Faith

Double Faith (dvoeverie) in Slavic Culture Double faith is a term used to describe the coexistence of Christian beliefs and practices with pre-Christian, pagan traditions among Slavic peoples, particularly after the Christianization of Rus' in 988. The basic idea is that people could identify as Christians while continuing to practice rituals, observe customs, and believe in supernatural forces inherited from their pre-Christian past.
