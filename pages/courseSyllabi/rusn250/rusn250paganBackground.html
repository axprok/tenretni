Pagan Festivals and their Christian Counterparts

Folk Festival Christian Counterpart Relationship
Koliada (aka Yuletide) Christmas Winter caroling and folk customs continued during the Christmas season.
Maslenitsa (Shrovetide) Forgiveness Sunday (Прощёное воскресенье) The final week before Great Lent, traditionally celebrated with blini(pancakes) and festivities.
Mermaid Week (aka Green Week Trinity Sunday (Троица) Seasonal traditions involving greenery and spirits became associated with the Trinity celebrations.
Ivan Kupala Day Nativity of St. John the Baptists Traditional fire, water, herb-gathering, and divination customs became associated with the saint's feast.
