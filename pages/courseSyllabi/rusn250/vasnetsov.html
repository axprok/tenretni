Viktor Vasnetsov and his Artistic Style

Viktor Vasnetsov (1848–1926) was one of the most influential Russian painters of the late 19th and early 20th centuries. He is particularly important for transforming Russian folklore, fairy tales, medieval legends, and national history into a distinctive artistic tradition. His work helped establish a national-romantic style in Russian art
