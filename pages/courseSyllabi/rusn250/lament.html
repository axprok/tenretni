Lament

A traditional ritual song expressing grief, often performed at weddings or funerals.

  • Funeral Laments: Widows and orphans wailed to help guide the soul, symbolize its departure, and communicate with the dead. These are considered the oldest form.
  • Wedding Laments: Brides performed ritual keening from betrothal through marriage to mourn their lost youth and symbolize entering a new, unfamiliar family.
  • Recruiting Laments: Women voiced deep sorrow and political protest against forced military conscription.
